Priority levels
ERROR — counts as a Jump on the sheet
LOW — yard/GPS drift: odometer 0, ≤ 10 miles while stopped, or drive ≤ 5 min
SPEED — implied driving speed exceeds 75 mph
LOCATION — location mismatch vs. status
SIGNATURE — driver signature missing for a day
Odometer rules
▸ DRIVING / PERSONAL / YARD — must increase in next event
▸ ON / OFF DUTY / SLEEPER BERTH — must not change by more than 10 miles
Location rules
▸ DRIVING — location must change (ignored if drive ≤ 15 min)
▸ ON / OFF DUTY / SLEEPER BERTH — location must not change
Day boundaries
Events are only ever compared with the next event on the same day, so a missing day in the logbook can't create a false violation.
Signature timing
Drivers certify at the end of the day, so today's log is N/A until signed — never Missing. Once the day has passed, unsigned becomes Missing; if it's signed later it becomes Miss/Corr and the day's Status turns Resolved.
No log for a date
A driver who never logged in produces no page for that day — recorded as Working = No with N/A across, up to and including today.
